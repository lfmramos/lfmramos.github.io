One of the greatest obstacles humanity has always faced is how to live harmoniously with biodiversity, despite needing it for a range of activities. One of the activities gaining the most importance nowadays is biotechnology. The challenge that presents itself is to produce economic wealth without degrading biological wealth. The aim of this work is to provide information on this impasse between the economy and environmental preservation. The importance of biodiversity and the use of biotechnologies is highlighted through a reflection on the rational use of this important development tool. One of the main conclusions reached is that environmental protection should not hinder the exploitation of the economic potential and the generation of intellectual property from biotechnology. Finally, perspectives are offered on how the management of these resources can simultaneously generate profits and the preservation of biodiversity.
