Although present in many legal documents for decades, the fight against discrimination, in its most varied forms, remains a constant challenge. The technological advances observed in recent years, despite serving to fight discrimination, sometimes they end up being the tools that enhance their damage. The use of decision-making algorithms based on Artificial Intelligence in the process of selecting and hiring personnel by organizations has revealed the damaging potential of this technology, by replicating – albeit involuntarily – the prejudices existing in society. This work aims to address the risks caused by the so-called “algorithmic bias”, as well as to indicate possible ways of mitigating the problem.
